SMALL COMMANDS. REAL CONSEQUENCES.

Four laws.
Plenty of leverage.

Chat changes bounded rules, never arbitrary code. A patch must leave an escape from its checkpoint before it enters the vote.

↓Gravity+

One cell of drift after a legal move. Change the direction, change where a move ends.

!patch gravity up|down|left|right|off
≈Ice+

Make designated ice tiles slippery or walkable. Slides stop at a wall or stable floor.

!patch ice on|off
◎Portals+

Normal pairs: 0↔1 / 2↔3.
Crossed pairs: 0↔3 / 1↔2.
The endpoint numbers make the change explicit.

!patch portals normal|crossed
▥Walls+

Open or close authored wall groups A and B. A wall can never close on the player.

!patch walls A|B open|closed
